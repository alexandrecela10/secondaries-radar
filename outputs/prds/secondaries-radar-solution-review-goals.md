# Goals: Secondaries Radar PRD (Solution Review, demo)

**Passing bar:** all criteria pass.

1. Every requirement has an ID and is testable by a command, a test or a visible output.
2. The problem table matches the opportunity analysis, one metric with a target per row.
3. Verdict, score, confidence, match and pension-score formulas are defined, including missing-metric and veto edge cases.
4. AI behaviour examples cover parsing, signals, chat and rulebook, each with Good, Bad and Reject cases.
5. The risk table covers every pipeline stage (collect, clean, match, compute, AI) plus use and publishing, each with detection and mitigation.
6. Out of scope lists every untackled problem with a reason.
7. Zero em dashes; no fabricated fact about a real organisation; no employer data.
8. Section 9 states objective criteria and a decision rule for choosing the collection architecture.
